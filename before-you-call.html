Before you call or visit

A listing gives you somewhere to start. The provider can tell you whether it serves your area, has an opening, or needs a referral. You can read these words straight from the page.

Write down one thing you need

Try a single sentence, like “I need groceries this week.” Have a place to write down a name, a time, and the next step. You can ask about the process before sharing personal details.

Start the conversation

“Hi, I’m calling about ___. I live in ___. Do you help people in my area? What’s the first step?”

For a food pantry, you might ask, “Can I come today? Do I need an appointment or a referral? Which entrance should I use?” If travel is difficult, ask whether you can start by phone.

If you need a moment

“Could you say that a little more slowly? I’m writing it down.”

You can ask for an interpreter, an accessible way to contact the office, or a written copy of the instructions. If you leave voicemail, say how and when it is safe to reach you.

Confirm one next step

“Let me check I have this right. I should come ___ at ___ and bring ___. Is that correct?”

Check the cost, address and documents before travelling. If you’re missing a document, ask whether they accept an alternative.

If that lead falls through

If the program is full, ask “When should I check back? Is there another place I could try today?” Ask for its name, direct number and whether you need a referral.

If a link is broken or a number is disconnected, try the organization’s official main office. For an error on this website, contact Single Step Forward with the listing name and what went wrong. Please leave out private case details. Contact the provider directly about appointments or eligibility.
