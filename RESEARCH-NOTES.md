# Research notes and unresolved operational checks

Checked November 18, 2026. These are research limitations, not claims that the providers are closed or unavailable.

## Chicago and suburban Cook County

Several City of Chicago service pages denied retrieval, and the CHI311 pages returned an application shell without usable service content. Their historical details were not silently treated as reverified. The refreshed guide uses All Chicago for housing navigation, Cook County Health for health access, Illinois domestic-abuse support, Illinois legal resources and other verified routes. The original entries remain available in the reference sheet and the refresh audit.

The historical Illinois WIC URL returned 404. CEDA’s current WIC route is included with clinic-level service-area confirmation. The older Illinois substance-use page did not expose enough usable content; SAMHSA’s treatment tools provide a verified wider route. Chicago senior-service navigation uses the Illinois Senior HelpLine. AgeOptions is in the suburban guide, not the Chicago guide.

The Anti-Cruelty Pet Resource Center page announced revised food-distribution rules effective November 12, 2026. The guide asks readers to check those rules and does not repeat a recurring event schedule that could become stale. Its Roseland-focused services and wider pet-food eligibility must not be conflated. The broader pet-owner directory is a separate route.

The Housing Authority of Cook County and Entry Point entries are restricted to the suburban guide. Oak Park Public Library is identified as an Oak Park service, not a countywide library entitlement. Housing Forward and Connections for the Homeless retain narrower service-area notes.

Cook County programme phone listings encountered during research were inconsistent in at least one legal-aid pathway; those disputed numbers were not added. The guides use separately verified legal services and their own contact routes.

## London

Some Barking and Dagenham, Barnet and Lambeth council pages blocked retrieval or returned bot-interruption screens. Those pages were not marked verified. Their official council directory links remain as navigation routes, supplemented by verified local providers: Citizens Advice and Barking Foodbank; Barnet Homes, Citizens Advice Barnet and Homeless Action in Barnet; Lambeth Larder and Brixton Advice Centre.

Homeless Action in Barnet offers a day-centre route, not overnight accommodation. Older source references to office addresses were not copied into the guide. Ask the service about its current access arrangements.

Hillingdon’s council navigation led to the `pre.hillingdon.gov.uk` host. The service content was readable there; recheck the preferred public URL before website integration. London Councils’ Freedom Pass link redirects to `freedompass.org`; the destination is recorded. TfL’s general discount page could not be read directly and was not used as a verified row; the Freedom Pass route has narrower, explicit eligibility.

The City of London cost-of-living page produced differing phone details between retrievals. Neither was carried into the guide. The guide links to the council’s current City Advice and support pages instead. City of London is kept separate from the other boroughs and from “London” as a regional label.

Council topic pages are marked as navigation routes, not as evidence that every linked grant or service is accepting applications. Where no dedicated local provider is listed for a topic, the guide uses a relevant England/UK route and states that scope.

## New Zealand

Some government, council and provider sites returned security blocks or empty application shells. These are access limitations, not proof of closure. Healthpoint provides the included health and mental-health service-finding routes; it is identified as a provider directory rather than a government page.

The old SPCA centre URL returned 404; the contact/centre route is used instead. The Immigration Advisers Authority register replaces an unreadable Citizens Advice Bureau page for immigration-adviser navigation. An adviser listing is not a promise of free advice.

Hamilton Christian Night Shelter could not be verified during this pass. It is not included as an active checked service. Work and Income’s housing route remains available in the Hamilton guide; Vinnies supplies a separately verified local food route. No shelter vacancy is claimed.

Tauranga Community Foodbank specifies a referral process. The guide points to that process rather than suggesting an unconfirmed walk-in collection. Family Works Otago lists foodbank, financial mentoring and family services; its guide asks the service to confirm the appropriate intake.

Transport concessions differ by network, card, age, residence and assessment. The guides link to current operator pages without hard-coding fare amounts or promising universal free travel. BUSIT’s concession page was reviewed through web retrieval after direct retrieval was blocked. AgeOptions was similarly reviewed through web retrieval for the US guide; both exceptions are recorded in the source registry.

NZ FUA identifiers are retained from the supplied geography reference. No new polygon import, address-matching rule or automatic council lookup has been built in this work. Provider scopes are recorded separately, and exact address eligibility remains a provider confirmation step.

## Maintenance

Prioritise emergency contacts, housing intake, food referrals and funding windows when rechecking before publication. Keep source-check dates distinct from human-review dates. Do not mark a page verified from its HTTP response alone: bot screens, application shells and unrelated redirects can return HTTP 200.

Only authored summaries and source metadata are included; full copied source pages are not redistributed. The current guide package changes no live website files.
