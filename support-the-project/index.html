This opens Ko-fi in a new tab. Any payment is optional and takes place on Ko-fi, not on Single Step Forward.

What support may fund

  • Domain registration and basic infrastructure
  • Source review, correction work, and responsibly expanding local guides
  • Accessibility, mobile usability, and educational resources
  • Potential future organizational formation and administrative costs

Funds available for project work are net of applicable payment and platform fees. We will not claim that every contributed dollar reaches a specific activity.

Editorial independence

Programs never pay to be listed, ranked, or recommended. Contributions do not purchase benefits, priority assistance, or eligibility. Resources are chosen for usefulness, source quality, and scope, not sponsor preference.

Legal and payment status

Single Step Forward is currently presented as an independent public-service project, not as a verified 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. No payment is taken on this website. The Single Step Forward Ko-fi page is hosted by Ko-fi; its checkout identifies available payment methods and relevant payment terms. For payment issues or refund requests, contact the project or follow the payment provider's applicable process. Payment questions: contact@singlestepforward.com.

Helping without contributing money

You can help by reporting outdated links, checking a program against its official source, and sharing an appropriate guide. Financial support is never required.
