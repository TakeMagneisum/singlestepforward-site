What this is

A local guide for complicated moments.

Help exists, but finding it often means knowing the right agency, phrase, form, or phone number. Single Step Forward reduces that search into one useful next action.

It is a navigation layer—not a government agency, emergency service, case manager, crowdfunding platform, or replacement for professional care.

Meet Dawn

A little guidance,
right when it helps.

Dawn is the friendly guide inside the directory. Tell her what is going on in everyday language and she will match it to practical, checked resources.

Did you know?

In this prototype, Dawn’s matching happens only on your device. Your words are not sent to a server, saved, scored, or used to build a profile.

Designed with care

Useful without becoming another obstacle.

Every choice should make it easier to understand what to do next—especially on a difficult day.

Safety before browsing

911 and 988 stay visible. Urgent language is routed to crisis options before ordinary directory results.

Official source first

We point to the agency or provider that owns the information, and label local, state, federal, and nonprofit sources.

Privacy by default

No login, no saved searches, no hidden profile, and no need to share a name to look for help.

How a listing earns trust

Checked links. Plain labels. Honest limits.

The larger vision

Local enough to be useful.
Simple enough to grow.

Liberty County is the starting point. The long-term model is a dependable front door for each community: local service knowledge, official pathways, timely public warnings, and human-friendly guidance in one place.

A future secure assistant can help people navigate forms and choices. It should never diagnose, decide eligibility, impersonate an agency, or replace emergency and professional services.

Help improve the map

Know a resource we should include?
