## Start here

Pick the one need that matters most today. Use the listed route, say where you are and what you need, and ask what happens next. You do not need to solve every problem at once.

For immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency, call 911. For suicide or emotional crisis support, call [988](tel:988?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline), text [988](sms:988?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline), or use the [988 website](https://988lifeline.org?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline). These are US routes.

## Before contacting a service

Ask: “Do you cover my address? Can I apply or be referred now? What documents do I need? Is there a cost or repayment? What should I do if you cannot help?” Mention court, eviction, shutoff or other deadlines early. Ask for an interpreter or accessible contact option when needed.

Share sensitive documents only through the provider’s confirmed intake channel. The listings do not confirm a vacancy, payment, appointment or entitlement. Phone fields are blank when a current number was not specifically verified; use the linked service route.
